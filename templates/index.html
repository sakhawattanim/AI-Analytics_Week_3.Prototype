TOPIC 1

What Happened vs. Why?

A descriptive statement tells you what changed. A diagnostic investigation asks which evidence can help explain that change.

QuestionWhat it helps you examine
Where did churn increase most?Segments or conditions where the change is concentrated.
What changed alongside churn?Operational or customer-experience indicators that moved at the same time.
Which explanation fits multiple pieces of evidence?Whether a proposed explanation is supported across different indicators.
TOPIC 2

Follow the Evidence

EvidenceWhat it showsDiagnostic use
2+ support contacts: 10.8% → 19.7%Churn is much higher among customers with more support contacts.Suggests a connection between service experience and churn.
Resolution time: 18 → 31 hoursCustomer issues took longer to resolve.Provides operational context for support-contact pattern.

Make a Careful Root-Cause Argument

PartYour jobExample structure
ClaimState explanation evidence supports.“The evidence points toward…”

ReasoningExplain why findings support claim.“Together, these findings suggest…”
